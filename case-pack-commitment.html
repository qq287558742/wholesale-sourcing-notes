Case Pack Is the Real Commitment

Published by DPJ Wholesale. Why unit cost is the weakest number in a wholesale catalog.

A cheap unit cost with a case pack of 48 is not a cheap buy

It is a commitment of shelf space, freight and working capital. Unit cost is the easiest number to compare and the least predictive; two SKUs at the same unit cost can differ by a factor of three in landed cost.

What to confirm before committing

  • Case pack — how many units arrive per order line.
  • Unit dimensions and weight — decides freight, shelf space and whether it ships alone or on a mixed pallet.
  • Recommended retail band — if the catalog omits it, the buyer has to work out the margin before deciding anything.
  • A consumable attached — the accessory restocks even when the main unit does not.
