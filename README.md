# Kitchen and Outdoor Sourcing Notes

What independent retailers re-buy in kitchen and outdoor categories, drawn from
aggregate order data across a 65,000-SKU wholesale catalog.

- Kitchen: measuring spoons and cups carry the highest repeat rate because they are
  lost rather than worn out. Mixing bowl sets with lids reorder at roughly twice the
  rate of sets without.
- Outdoor: the repeat purchase is a worn or lost part — tent stakes, guy lines,
  lanterns — not an upgrade.
- Patio and garden: bird feeders show the clearest seasonal curve; compost bins are a
  one-time purchase while the accelerator is the repeat line.
- What does not come back: decorative planters, which behave like fashion rather than
  garden equipment.
