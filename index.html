Kitchen and Outdoor Sourcing Notes

Published by DPJ Wholesale, a B2B wholesale catalog covering 65,000 consumer SKUs across kitchen, outdoor, pet and storage.

Kitchen: the SKUs that are lost, not worn out

Measuring spoons and cups carry the highest repeat rate in our kitchen category because they are lost rather than worn out. That makes replacement demand steady instead of seasonal.

Mixing bowls with lids

Sets with lids reorder at roughly twice the rate of sets without. The lid is not an accessory here; it is the feature that decides the repurchase.

Outdoor: parts, not upgrades

  • Replacement tent stakes and guy lines — the classic lost item, restocked every season.
  • Camp cookware that nests — nesting decides pack volume, and pack volume decides whether the set comes along.
  • Portable lanterns — cheap, universally gifted, replaced more often than upgraded.

Patio and garden: a rhythm, not a forecast

Bird feeders show the clearest seasonal curve in the catalog — a spring peak, a smaller autumn peak, and almost nothing between. Compost bins are a one-time purchase; the accelerator is the repeat line. Tree stakes and ties are replaced every season as the ties degrade.

What does not come back

Decorative planters. They sell on appearance and the buyer rarely wants the same design twice, so they behave like fashion rather than like garden equipment.
