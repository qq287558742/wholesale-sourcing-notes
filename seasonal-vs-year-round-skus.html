Seasonal vs Year-Round SKUs

Published by DPJ Wholesale. Which demand curves actually repeat, and which are a bet with a deadline.

Structure repeats; fashion does not

Bird feeders show the clearest seasonal curve we carry — a spring peak, a smaller autumn peak, almost nothing between. That is structure: it repeats, so it can be planned and reordered against.

Decorative planters look similar on a chart for one season and then do not repeat, because the buyer rarely wants the same design twice. That is fashion, and it behaves like apparel.

One-time purchase vs repeat line

Compost bins are bought once. The accelerator is bought repeatedly. Confusing the two is how a retailer ends up holding stock in the item that sells once and running out of the one that sells every month.
